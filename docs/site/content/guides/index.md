# Choose your channel

The same client covers three ways onto the wire. They share the protocol
engine, not the audio device or the evidence they leave behind.

- [[guides/session|Sessions and sources]]: join a server, pick a source, read the RESULT line.
- [[guides/live-audio|Live audio]]: capture the local device and play the peer mix.
- [[guides/demo|The end-to-end demo]]: two clients, a reference peer, and a real server.

For exact flags and defaults, use [[reference/cli|the command reference]].
For what is actually on the wire, use [[reference/protocol|the protocol notes]].
